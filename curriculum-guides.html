Curriculum Guides

Understanding the different educational curricula available in UAE schools

British Curriculum (GCSE/A-Levels)

The British curriculum follows the National Curriculum of England, leading to IGCSE and A-Level qualifications.

  • Early Years Foundation Stage (Ages 3-5)
  • Key Stages 1-4 (Ages 5-16)
  • A-Levels (Ages 16-18)
  • Focus on analytical thinking and subject specialization
Foundation Stage Primary Secondary Sixth Form

American Curriculum

Based on US educational standards, leading to an American High School Diploma with optional Advanced Placement (AP) courses.

  • Elementary School (Grades KG-5)
  • Middle School (Grades 6-8)
  • High School (Grades 9-12)
  • Wide range of extracurricular activities
Elementary Middle School High School

International Baccalaureate (IB)

A globally recognized program emphasizing international mindedness and critical thinking.

  • Primary Years Programme (Ages 3-12)
  • Middle Years Programme (Ages 11-16)
  • Diploma Programme (Ages 16-19)
  • Emphasis on research and independent learning
PYP MYP DP

Additional Considerations

  • All curricula include Arabic language and Islamic studies as per UAE requirements
  • Schools often offer additional language options
  • Extra-curricular activities vary by school
  • Different fee structures apply to different curricula
  • Consider your child's learning style and future educational goals
