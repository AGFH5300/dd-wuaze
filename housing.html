Renting Process

  1. Research areas and properties
  2. Contact real estate agents
  3. View properties
  4. Submit required documents:
    • Passport copy
    • Visa copy
    • Emirates ID
    • Proof of income/employment
  5. Sign tenancy contract
  6. Pay security deposit and rent
  7. Register Ejari (Dubai) or Tawtheeq (Abu Dhabi)
  8. Set up utilities
