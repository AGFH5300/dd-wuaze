Important Documents

Essential documentation you need for living in the UAE

Entry Visa

Your entry visa is your first step to entering the UAE. Here's what you need to know:

  • Valid for 30-90 days depending on type
  • Must be sponsored by employer, family member, or tourism company
  • Can be extended for additional fee
  • Required for most nationalities

Residence Visa

After entering the UAE, you'll need to obtain a residence visa. Here's the process:

  1. Medical fitness test at approved center
  2. Emirates ID application
  3. Visa stamping in passport
  4. Registration with local authorities

Emirates ID

Your Emirates ID is your primary identification document in the UAE. Key points:

  • Mandatory for all UAE residents
  • Used for government services
  • Required for banking and telecommunications
  • Contains biometric information

Additional Important Documents

  • Driving License (if applicable)
  • Labor Card (for employed residents)
  • Marriage Certificate (if applicable, attested)
  • Educational Certificates (attested)
  • Insurance Documents
