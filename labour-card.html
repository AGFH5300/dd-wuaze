Labour Card in the UAE

Your guide to understanding the labour card system, application process, and employee rights in the UAE.

Labour Card Overview

A labour card is a work permit issued by the UAE government, required for all foreign employees working in the UAE. It serves as proof of employment and is required for various processes, including obtaining a visa and healthcare access. Employers must apply for a labour card on behalf of their employees.

How to Apply for a Labour Card

To apply for a labour card, your employer must submit an application to the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). The application includes documents such as the employment contract, passport copies, and proof of medical fitness. The process may take several weeks to complete, depending on the type of work and the employer's compliance with regulations.

Labour Card Renewal

Labour cards are valid for a period of 1 to 3 years, depending on the contract. To renew your labour card, you must undergo a renewal process with the assistance of your employer. The renewal process usually involves submitting updated documents, including a renewed employment contract and proof of continued medical fitness.

Employee Rights under the Labour Card

The labour card guarantees your rights as an employee in the UAE, including access to health insurance, employment benefits, and legal protections. Employers must adhere to labour laws, which include ensuring proper working conditions and providing employees with rights such as paid leave, end-of-service benefits, and wage protection.
