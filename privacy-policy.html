Privacy Policy

Last updated: Wed 4 Dec 2024

Introduction

Welcome to DD. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services related to expatriate life in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register on our website
  • Fill out contact forms
  • Subscribe to newsletters
  • Submit inquiries about UAE relocation
  • Create user profiles
  • Participate in forums or discussions

This information may include:

  • Name and email address
  • Contact information
  • Nationality and passport details
  • Employment information
  • Residential status in the UAE
  • Family information (if relevant for visa purposes)

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Usage data
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide accurate UAE relocation and lifestyle information
  • Process your inquiries and requests
  • Send newsletters and updates about UAE living
  • Improve our website content and services
  • Comply with UAE legal requirements
  • Protect our website and users
  • Analyze website performance and user behavior

Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate security measures in accordance with UAE Federal Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection and ensure:

  • Secure storage of personal information
  • Protection against unauthorized access
  • Regular security updates
  • Compliance with UAE data protection regulations

Third-Party Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in website operation
  • UAE government authorities when legally required
  • Third-party partners who help provide relocation services
  • Analytics and advertising partners

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights

Under UAE data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request corrections to your data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • File complaints about data handling

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. Parents can contact us to remove any inadvertently collected information about their children.

Cookies Policy

We use cookies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Enhance user experience

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

Contact Information

Governing Law
